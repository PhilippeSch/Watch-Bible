## Short version

**Watch Bible collects no data.** The app has no server, no account, no network code and no third-party components. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

## No data collection, no tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no software development kits from other companies. It does not read the advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites. The developer receives no information about you or about your use of the app.

## No network access

All twelve Bible translations ship inside the app as a read-only database. The app contains no network code whatsoever; in airplane mode it works exactly as it does otherwise.

## No permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It has no access to location, health data, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera or notifications.

## What is stored on your watch

Your settings and your reading position remain on your Apple Watch alone, in the app's own storage area:

- the chosen Bible translation, the random-verse mode and the chosen topic
- appearance (day/night, the two switching times), text size, haptic feedback
- the passage you last read, so the app can offer to continue there
- the verse of the day currently shown by the complication

This information never leaves the device. It is not sent to the developer, is not synchronised to iCloud and is not shared with anyone. Deleting the app deletes it along with the app.

## Children

Since the app collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

## Changes

Should this policy ever change, the new version will appear at this address with a new date. As long as the app collects no data, there is nothing here that could require substantive change.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue in the [Watch Bible repository on GitHub](https://github.com/PhilippeSch/Watch-Bible/issues).

Developer: Philippe Scheuber, Switzerland
